Privacy Policy

Cloud Motion Lab · Last updated May 2, 2026

Pikd is a tier list maker built to keep your data on your device. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not collect.

What we collect

Nothing personal. The app does not require an account. It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or device identifiers for advertising purposes.

Tier lists you create

Tier lists, item names, photos you add, and your ranking choices stay on your device. They are not transmitted to a server, not stored in the cloud, and not shared with any third party.

Photos

If you choose to add photos to a tier list from your photo library, the app reads only the images you explicitly select. Photos remain on your device. The app never accesses your full photo library without your permission.

Anonymous analytics

The app uses TelemetryDeck for anonymous, privacy-preserving usage analytics. TelemetryDeck collects coarse, non-identifying signals (such as which screen was viewed, whether a tier list was started, app version, device type, country) hashed with a daily rotating salt. No persistent device identifier is stored. You can read TelemetryDeck's privacy approach at telemetrydeck.com/privacy.

In-app purchase

Pikd offers an optional Pro upgrade as a weekly auto-renewing subscription with an introductory free trial, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. The app receives only an Apple-issued receipt confirming entitlement. We do not see your Apple ID, your payment details, or your billing address. RevenueCat (privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy) is used to validate Apple receipts; the only data shared is the anonymous Apple receipt and an anonymous app-user identifier.

What we do not do

  • No third-party advertising networks
  • No tracking across apps or websites (we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track)
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of any data
  • No background location, no contacts, no microphone

Children

Pikd is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content. The app is suitable for general use and complies with COPPA by collecting no personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, the "Last updated" date above will move and we will note the change in the app's What's New on its next release.

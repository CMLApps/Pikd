What the app does

Pikd is a tier list maker. You drag items (emoji, photos, or text) into ranked tiers (S, A, B, C, D, F) to express your preferences and share the result. The app includes built-in category libraries, a randomizer (Chaos Spin) that picks a category and prompt for you, and tools to export and share completed lists.

Free vs Pro

Pikd is free to download. The free tier includes one custom tier list, access to non-Pro library categories, and the standard tier editor. The optional Pro upgrade unlocks unlimited custom tier lists, the full library of Pro-gated categories, and the full Chaos Spin set.
